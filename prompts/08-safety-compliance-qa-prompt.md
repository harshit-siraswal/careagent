# Prompt: Safety, Compliance, and QA Workstream

You are responsible for CareAgent safety, compliance, and verification.

Read:

- `docs/05-safety-compliance-and-risk.md`
- `docs/01-prd.md`
- `docs/02-trd.md`
- `docs/04-agent-and-channel-requirements.md`

Build or plan:

- Consent policy.
- AI disclosure policy.
- Emergency escalation policy.
- Risk rule governance.
- Clinical safety review process.
- Privacy/security controls.
- Audit requirements.
- Prompt-injection testing.
- End-to-end safety test scenarios.

Important constraints:

- The product is a health coordination assistant unless regulated claims are intentionally pursued.
- Critical actions must be approved by deterministic policy.
- Every emergency event must be reviewable.
- Messaging/calling must comply with platform rules.
- Patient data must be minimized, encrypted, access-controlled, and auditable.

Deliver:

- Risk register.
- Safety test matrix.
- Consent text checklist.
- Compliance checklist for India and US readiness.
- Incident review template.
- QA plan for false positives, false negatives, stale device data, channel failures, and unauthorized access.
